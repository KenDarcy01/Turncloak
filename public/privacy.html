Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026.

Turncloak is a party game. It is built to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

What we store

  • A guest id. When you host or join a game, your device signs in anonymously and gets a random id. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple or Google account.
  • Game data. The names you type for players, roles, votes, chat between Traitors during the night, suspicion notes and scores. This is stored on our game server (Supabase) so every phone at the table sees the same game.
  • On your device. Your sign-in and a list of recent games, so you can pick a game up again.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or payment details, and we do not sell or share data with anyone. Data is used only to run the game.

Deleting your data

Tap Reset my data on the home screen. This deletes your guest id from the server, together with every game you hosted and your seats in other games, and clears everything saved on your device. A game someone else hosted belongs to them, so the name you used may stay in that game's history until they reset their own data.

Children

Turncloak is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/KenDarcy01/turncloak/issues.
