Turncloak help

Turncloak is a party game of loyalty and betrayal for 4 to 22 players. One screen hosts the game, ideally on a TV, and everyone plays on their own phone.

Starting a game

  1. On the host screen, tap Host a game. A four letter code appears.
  2. Everyone else opens Turncloak, taps Join and enters the code, or scans the QR code.
  3. Short of players? Add bots. They vote, plot and argue at the Round Table.

Common problems

  • A phone dropped out. Open Turncloak again and tap the game under "Continue a game".
  • Someone needs a new phone. On the host screen, open Players and free their seat, then they join with the code and tap their name.
  • Deleting your data. Tap "Reset my data" on the home screen. See the privacy policy.
