How the evening runs

You plug a laptop into the screen and the game does the hosting. Nobody has to learn rules in advance.

I

Everyone joins

Colleagues scan the QR code on the screen with their phones. Each phone secretly shows who is a Truecloak and who is a Turncloak.

II

Missions

Quick team games built for a work party earn gold for the prize pot. The Turncloaks play along, and sometimes quietly sabotage.

III

The Round Table

Accuse, defend, then vote on your phones. Whoever gets the most votes is banished and their role is revealed to the room.

IV

Night falls

The Turncloaks pick someone to murder (out of the game, not off the payroll). At breakfast the room finds out who.

The Office Party mission pack

23 missions written for colleagues: work-safe, no touching, nobody has to drink, and anyone can pass their turn. A few favourites:

  • The BuzzwordOne player must slip “synergy” into chat about Christmas plans without getting caught.
  • Reply AllAn all-staff email is whispered round the room. What reaches the last desk?
  • The Christmas BonusA crew carries the bonus envelopes on their phones. Only a Turncloak can lighten them.
  • Decode the AcronymEveryone says KPI and SLA. Can the team say what they stand for?
  • The Org ChartLine up by how long you have worked here, in total silence.
  • The Annual ReviewTwo true things and one invention about your year. Spot the fiction.
  • The Office ChoirHum a Christmas song. No words, and the team has one guess.
  • The Group OrderEveryone picks on their phone. Anyone who never agrees with the team starts to look suspicious.

Why organisers pick it

It mixes the room

The new starter ends up banishing the finance director. People talk to colleagues they never normally sit near.

Nothing to prepare

No scripts, costumes, printing or props. Open the page, put it on the screen, and start when the starters are cleared.

Works in any venue

A function room, a restaurant’s private room or the office itself. If there is a screen and phone signal, you are set.

Questions

We have more than 22 people.

Run two or more tables at once, each with its own screen and game code. Each table plays its own game, and you can compare prize pots at the end.

Does everyone need to download an app?

No. Players join in their phone’s browser by scanning the QR code. The host only needs a laptop with a browser, connected to a TV or projector.

Do we need Wi-Fi?

Every phone needs an internet connection, through Wi-Fi or mobile data. The game uses very little data.

What if we are short of players?

Bots can fill empty seats. They vote, plot and argue at the Round Table and play the phone games.

Is it work-appropriate?

Yes. The “murders” are announced at breakfast in the game and nothing is graphic. The office missions avoid physical contact, personal questions and drinking, and anyone can pass.

Can we try it before the party?

Yes. The demo lets one person play a whole game against bots, on a laptop or a phone.
